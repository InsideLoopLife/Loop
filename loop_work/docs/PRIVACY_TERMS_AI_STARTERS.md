## Privacy notice summary

Inside LOOP collects information you choose to add about your account, household, food, health, routines, preferences and wealth tracking. We use this to provide dashboards, insights, AI-assisted summaries, household sharing, reminders and beta support.

We do not sell personal health or financial data.

For privacy requests, contact: privacy@insideloop.life.

## Terms summary

Inside LOOP is a tracking and insight platform. It is not a medical device, financial adviser, investment adviser or emergency service.

Do not use Inside LOOP for urgent medical or financial emergencies.

## AI disclaimer

Inside LOOP may use AI to help summarise and explain information. AI outputs may be inaccurate, incomplete or unsuitable for your circumstances.

Health AI does not diagnose, prescribe or treat conditions. Wealth AI does not provide regulated financial advice, personalised investment recommendations or guaranteed outcomes.

## Delete account wording

You can request deletion from your account settings. If you confirm deletion by typing `DELETE`, Inside LOOP will purge your core profile, food, health, household and wealth data where it is linked to your account. Minimal operational logs may be retained where necessary for security, legal or abuse-prevention purposes.
