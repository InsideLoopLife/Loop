# Research questions for the LOOP savings optimiser

Use these questions to decide what the optimiser is allowed to infer, what it must ask, and what it should explain before ranking an action.

## Goal and liquidity

- What is this money ultimately for, and when might it be needed?
- What amount must remain available instantly?
- How much notice or lock-in is genuinely acceptable?
- Are withdrawals likely to be rare, seasonal or unpredictable?
- Is capital certainty essential, or can some money sit in investments instead of cash?

## Emergency resilience

- How many months of essential household spending should be held as an emergency fund: three, six or another number?
- Which income is least secure, and how quickly could it stop?
- Are there known large costs in the next 12–24 months?
- Should the emergency fund be household-wide or allocated to one person?

## Tax position

- Whose legal ownership does each account belong to?
- What is each owner’s expected taxable income for the tax year, including savings interest?
- Is the user Scottish, UK-rest-of-UK, non-UK resident or subject to another tax treatment?
- How much ISA allowance has already been used outside LOOP?
- Is the account flexible, and were any ISA withdrawals made that can be replaced?
- Is interest paid monthly, annually or at maturity, and in which tax year is it taxable?

## Eligibility and provider relationships

- Is the user already a current-account, mortgage or savings customer of the provider?
- Are there postcode, age, employment or membership restrictions?
- Does a linked current account or monthly funding requirement create hidden friction?
- Is the account genuinely available through the user’s intended application channel?

## Rate comparison quality

- Is the compared product the same access type and term?
- Is the headline rate temporary, tiered or dependent on a bonus?
- What balance receives the advertised rate?
- Are there withdrawal penalties, loss-of-interest rules or monthly deposit caps?
- What is the expected net gain after tax, fees and the user’s likely behaviour?
- How fresh and authoritative is each rate source?

## FSCS and concentration risk

- Which brands share the same banking licence?
- How much protected cash is already held across that group?
- Is temporary high-balance protection relevant?
- Would a higher rate create an unacceptable concentration above the protection limit?

## Household decision-making

- Is the recommendation for one person, a joint account or the household as a whole?
- Can one household member see the balance but not move or edit it?
- Should reallocating money require the owner’s approval?
- Are goals and emergency reserves shared or private?

## Financial Flow and affordability

- Is the apparent monthly capacity stable or caused by missing spending lines?
- Are annual bills, childcare, debt and irregular costs already reserved?
- Should an action reduce debt before increasing cash savings?
- How much of the “unassigned equity” is genuinely spare rather than awaiting allocation?

## Behaviour and action friction

- Will the user actually switch for the estimated benefit?
- What minimum annual gain should trigger an alert?
- How many actions should LOOP present at once?
- Does the user prefer automatic monitoring, a monthly digest or urgent alerts only?
- Should LOOP recommend a partial move rather than closing an account?

## Evidence and explainability

- Can every score component be traced to a field, rule and source timestamp?
- What missing data materially changes the answer?
- What assumptions did LOOP make, and can the user overwrite them?
- What would cause the ranking to change?
- How should confidence fall when a balance, rate or eligibility fact becomes stale?

## Forward-looking events

- Is a fixed term, bonus rate, ISA tax year or mortgage deal ending soon?
- Is an expected bonus, house purchase, school cost, maternity change or retirement date approaching?
- Would moving money now conflict with a known future need?
- Should LOOP reserve cash for upcoming pot targets before suggesting a rate switch?

## Product and compliance boundary

- Is the output generic information, a comparison, a personalised ranking or regulated advice?
- Does the wording avoid claiming approval, guaranteed eligibility or “best for you”?
- When must LOOP route the user to an authorised adviser or lender-level process?
- What evidence must be preserved to show what the user saw and why?
